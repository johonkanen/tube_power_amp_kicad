### Transcription rules (for the sheet sessions)

- **Symbols and values**: only `tube_power_amp:` symbols, exactly the `lib_id` and `value` of
  `lib/part_map.csv` per refdes (`_common.sym(ref)`, `sh_value(ref)`; C5's value is `10µ/400V`).
  Refdes = the `refdes` column: `P6` (`+6`), `N6` (`-6`), `HT_P6` (`HT+6`), `HT_N6` (`HT -6`),
  `HT_0` (`HT-0`), `VA_GND`, `PI_GND` (`P.I.GND`), `VS_PRIME` (`VS'`), `IN6_7`, `IN8_9`,
  `VA_PRIME` (`VA'`). Terminals with `_common.term()` (never `sheetkit.terminal`: wrong value,
  and it would place VA' on the board symbol).
- **Net names = IPC names** (`reference/pins_by_net.md`), whatever the PDF writes. The named nets:
  - **`HT +6`** (with the space; sheets 1 and 2: U8-2, U9-2, P6, HT_P6, R17-1, VCC) → **global
    label** `HT +6` on both sheets;
  - **`B-`** (sheets 2 and 3: Q1-3, Q3-3, R18-2, R24-1, R25-2, C5 `-`) → global label `B-`;

    label `OTGND`; **sheet 3 writes `otgnd`: same net, label it `OTGND`** (KiCad names are
    case-sensitive);
  - **`VS'`** (sheet 1 only: STANDBY, R13-R16 pin 2) → local label `VS'` on sheet 1.
  - All other nets are Proteus auto-names (`#000xx`): plain wires. None crosses a sheet, there
    are no single-pin nets and no N/C pads.
- **Terminal refdes are not net names** (Phase 0 notes): the terminal `VS'` (`VS_PRIME`, sheet 2,

How the sheets were drawn:

- **Coordinates from the PDF.** The pages rendered at 200 dpi have 0.127 mm pixels, so 10 px = one
  1.27 mm grid unit: `_common.P(x, y)` takes a render position in 10 px units and returns the
  sheet point (A3 sheet, shifted by 40 / 30 grid units). Sheets 2 and 3 follow the PDF 1:1.
- **Sheet 1 differs from the PDF drawing in two places** (same nets, noted on the sheet):
  1. KiCad's EL34 symbol has G3 (pin 1) on the right and the cathode (pin 8) at the bottom, where
     the Proteus symbol has 1, 5 and 8 on the left. The G3–cathode link and the cathode row (1R
     resistor, VBx terminal) therefore run below each tube instead of beside it.
  2. The heaters are unit 2 of each tube (KiCad draws the whole envelope for it), placed as a block
     of their own at the right of the sheet with the five heater terminals (`+6`, `HT+6`, `HT-0`,
     `-6`, `HT -6`) and the `HT +6` global label, instead of at the tubes.
- `_common.tube_channel()` draws one of the four channels (coupling C, grid stop, bias R + BIASx,
  tube unit 1, G3–K link, cathode 1R + VBx, screen R); `_common.place_mirrored()` places Q2 mirrored
  (p2k candidate 6); `_common.term()` got its own text justification (p2k candidate 7).
- **Symbol change during transcription:** EL34 anode (pin 3) `output` → `passive` in
  `tools/gen_symbols.py`. The tubes work in parallel pairs (U8 ∥ U9, U6 ∥ U7), and ERC reports two
  connected `output` pins as a `pin_to_pin` **error** (2 errors before the change). The smoke test
  of the preparation had only U6, so it did not show. Pin numbers and names unchanged.

## Findings about the original design

(Report here; don't fix.)

1. **The `VS'` terminal is not on the `VS'` net.** The bias regulator's divider input (sheet 2,
   terminal `VS'` → R19) is net `#00043`; the net named `VS'` (sheet 1 label: STANDBY, R13-R16
   screen resistors) is a different net. Proteus terminal parts do not join nets by their
   refdes, so on the board the two are separate pads (presumably joined by an external wire).
   Reproduced as is; worth confirming against the built amplifier.
2. **OT and VA' are not on the PCB**: the output transformer (1650TA) and the VA' terminal are
   drawn on sheet 1 but have no layout part; OT1/OT2 are the board's OT connections.
3. **`VCC` is the heater rail**: the terminal `VCC` and R17 (bias regulator supply) are on net
   `HT +6` together with `+6`, `HT+6` and U8/U9 pin 2.
4. Export quirks (no effect on the conversion): Mechanical 1's X2 FileFunction is
   `NonPlated,1,2,NPTH` although it holds only the profile; the solder resist files are negative
   polarity.
5. **Pinouts are standard** (Phase 1 preparation): the Proteus pin ids of EL34 (octal), TIPL760
   (TO-220 B C E), MPSA92 (TO-92 E B C), the diodes (A/K) and the electrolytics (+/-) equal the
   KiCad standard symbols and the datasheet pinouts. Phase 2 still checks them with the fit.
6. **OT has five secondary pins drawn unconnected** (S1-S5 of the Proteus 1650TA model,
   TRAN2P5S) and the primary centre tap goes only to the off-board terminal VA': the speaker
   wiring and the anode supply are outside the schematic.
7. Proteus device vs value: D5's value is `22V` on the device 1N5933B (a 22 V / 1.5 W zener, BV=22
   in the instance: consistent). R24/R25 use the
   METALFILM1K device with the 2 W package; C5 (`10µ/400V`) sits on the B- bias rail, C6 is 100u
   (device HITEMP100U100V, a 100 V part) on VREF. No action; values are taken from the schematic.

8. **Heater wiring is two series pairs** (Phase 1 transcription): `HT +6` → U8 and U9 heaters
   (pin 2 → 7) → net #00004 (terminal `HT-0`) → U6 and U7 heaters (pin 2 → 7) → net #00010
   (terminals `-6`, `HT -6`). Each rail has two terminals except the centre (`HT-0`). As drawn in
   the PDF; reproduced.
9. **Nothing else found on the three sheets**: every PDF connection agrees with the IPC netlist
   (compare 0 wrong-net pins without any net alias), the PDF values equal the part map, and no
   pin is drawn unconnected except pin 6 of the tubes and the OT secondaries.

10. **Octal socket pin numbering is mirrored against the octal standard** (Phase 2). The Proteus
   `8-PIN BELTON OCTAL` pads run 1 → 8 clockwise seen from the component (top) side; the standard
   (and KiCad's `Valve_Octal`) runs clockwise seen from the pins, i.e. from the solder side of a
   top-mounted socket. Proteus places U6-U9 on the top (Top Silk / Top Assembly). With a socket
   mounted on the top side, no rotation makes it work: e.g. tube pin 3 (anode) would land on the pad
   without a net. Since the amplifier was built, the sockets were most likely **mounted on the solder
   side** (tubes through the chassis below the board; then the pattern is correct), or the board was
   wired differently. **Please confirm with the built amplifier** which side the sockets are on. The
   conversion keeps the Proteus pads, ids and top side (copper identical either way); if the sockets
   are on the solder side, Phase 5 can move the socket footprint to B (silk / courtyard / 3D model)
   with standard numbering. Pitch circle 17.20 mm (Belton datasheet sockets are typically ~17.45 mm;
   it fitted in practice).
11. Smaller Proteus details seen in Phase 2: Q1's three pads are all square (KiCad: pad 1 only); the
   22n Xicon film pads are 9.70 mm apart (not a 10 / 10.16 grid); C6 (ELEC-RAD20) is P5.08 while C5
   is P5.00; the TO-92 pitch is 2.032 mm (80 mil).

12. **VB6-VB9 terminal pads overlap the octal socket body outline** (Phase 3 DRC). Each cathode-sense
   terminal sits 13.05 / 1.9 mm from its socket centre, 0.07 mm outside the 21 mm square body (Proteus
   Top Assembly, at 45°): its 3.048 mm pad reaches under the socket flange. Harmless if the sockets are
   on the solder side (Findings 10) or the terminal is a wire lead; on a top-mounted socket check the
   flange clearance. Reproduced as is.
13. **Proteus rules as built** (Phase 3): 15 mil (0.381 mm) smallest copper gap, 20 mil (0.508 mm)
   thinnest track, ≥ 0.635 mm copper to the board edge – generous; the KiCad pads keep ≥ 0.47 mm.

## p2k candidates

(Shared-tool limitations met on this board, with the board-local workaround used.)

1. **`p2k.pdsprj` drops non-ASCII values.** C5's value is `10µ/400V` with a Latin-1 `µ` (0xB5)
   in ROOT.DSN and ROOT.LYT. `dsn_values` returns `None` for it (its string test accepts bytes
   32-126 only) and `lyt_parts` misses the C5 record (`_strings` splits the run at 0xB5).
   Workaround (Phase 1, done): `LATIN1_PARTS` in `tools/make_part_map.py` gives C5's value (PDF)
   and its device `10µ/400V` / package `RAD ELEC R5 D10` (ROOT.LYT bytes); the device name has the
   µ too.
   The shared tool would need: decode Latin-1 strings (0xA0-0xFF) in `pstr` and `_strings`.
2. `p2k ipc356` prints "Net aliases: ." in `pin_nets_by_component.md` when `NET_ALIASES` is
   empty (cosmetic), and its index groups `HT_0` under `HT_`.
3. **`p2k.sheetkit.terminal` hard-codes symbol `PIN` and value "PIN".** This board has two
   terminal devices (TERMINAL, PIN) and an off-board terminal (VA', symbol `PIN_OFFBOARD`).
   Workaround: `term()` in `tools/sheets/_common.py` (same placement, symbol and value from
   the part map). The shared tool would need: `symbol=` and `value=` parameters.
4. **`p2k.pdsprj.dsn_pinouts` misses the pin definitions of library devices.** It parses only
   `*PINOUT <package>` blocks (here: EL34 octal, terminals, electrolytics); the device symbols'
   own pins (`$PINSHORT <name> <number>` / `$PINDEFAULT <name>` records before each `{*DEVICE}`
   block: TIPL760 B1 C2 E3, MPSA92 E1 B2 C3, 1N4001 A K, 1650TA P1 P2 CT S1-S5) are not read, and
   its `device` attribution is unreliable (most entries `None`, `1N5933B` attached to the
   `SOLEN 1U` pinout, `PIN` to `G5V-2(HI)`). Read by hand here. The shared tool would need: a
   reader for the `$PIN*` records grouped by the following `{*DEVICE}` block (with its DESC).
5. **Part-map generator** (`tools/make_part_map.py`) is the tube_psu script plus `OFF_BOARD`
   parts and `LATIN1_PARTS` overrides; a shared `p2k.part_map` with config keys
   (`SYMBOL_BY_DEVICE`, `PAGE_OVERRIDES`, `OFF_BOARD`, value overrides) would serve the preamp and
   PI/FX boards too.
6. **`p2k.sch_builder.Sheet.place` cannot mirror a symbol.** Q2 (PNP, base left, emitter up)
   needs the Q_PNP_EBC symbol mirrored about the X axis. Workaround: `place_mirrored()` in
   `tools/sheets/_common.py` (calls `place` at rotation 0, inserts `(mirror x|y)` into the node
   and mirrors the returned pin points). The shared tool would need: a `mirror=` parameter of
   `place` (pin points and default field positions mirrored). The preamp and PI/FX boards will
   have mirrored transistors / triodes too.
7. **`sheetkit.terminal` texts for 'left' / 'right' terminals** are justified towards the circle
   in KiCad 10.0.7 (kicad-cli render): the refdes and value are drawn over the terminal circle
   and wire. `term()` in `_common.py` uses the opposite justification (`"left"` for direction
   right, `"right"` for left), which renders the texts beside the circle, away from the wire. The
   shared helper would need the same swap (check with the ac_psu / tube_psu renders).
8. **Multi-unit symbols whose unit 0 graphics are the whole body** (KiCad `Valve:*`: the envelope
   is drawn for the heater unit too) cannot be placed on top of unit 1 as Proteus draws them.
   Board-local answer: a heater block per sheet. For the preamp / PI boards (ECC83 = 3 units)
   decide the same way up front and write it into the transcription rules.
9. **ERC pin types of the KiCad valve symbols**: anode `output`, heater / cathode `power_in`.
   Parallel tubes give `pin_to_pin` errors and passive-only nets "undriven" errors. Here all
   are set to `passive` in `tools/gen_symbols.py`; a `symgen` helper (`passive_pins=`) or a
   playbook note would save the next board the round trip.
10. `compare_netlists` prints "IPC nets touched 47, complete 47" on a 43-net board: the four
   pads without a net (U6-U9 pin 6) are counted as nets of their own (cosmetic).

11. **`ipc356` checks `TRUNCATED_NETS` by the KiCad refdes and ignores unknown keys.** The entry
   `"P.I.GND"` (Proteus name; KiCad `PI_GND`) was never checked (negative test: wrong net → still OK).
   Fixed here (key `PI_GND`, comment in p2k_config.py). The shared tool would need: accept the Proteus
   or the KiCad name, and fail on a key that matches no restored refdes (found by the PI/FX session).
12. **`footprints.run` does not write `kicad/fp-lib-table`.** Board-local: `fp_lib_table()` in
   `tools/gen_footprints.py` writes / `--check`s it (tube_psu has a hand-written one). The shared tool
   would need: write the project fp-lib-table with the library (as make_skeleton writes sym-lib-table).
13. **No shared pin-order check.** The playbook asks for a fit of the *unmoved* base footprint with the
   renumbering; here `tools/check_pin_order.py` does it (both mirror states, expectations, ideal octal,
   `--render` overlay of the KiCad graphics on the Proteus silk). Two-pad and inline parts fit either
   way, so the body marker (band, flat, tab, `+`) has to be looked at; a shared `p2k.pin_order` with a
   per-part `(base, renumber)` list and the render would serve the preamp / PI boards.
14. `footprints` `body_poly` takes fixed coordinates; the octal body here is read from the Top Assembly
   gerber at generation time (`octal_body()`). A spec key like `body_from=("Top Assembly", refdes)`
   (closed loop around the part) would avoid the board code (tube_psu's silk_bodies.py is the same idea).

15. **No shared gap measurement.** `tools/measure_gaps.py` is a verbatim copy of tube_psu's board
   script (smallest different-net gap per item-kind pair on the Proteus geometry, copper to outline,
   drill to drill; the input for `PCB_RULES`). A `p2k.measure_gaps` would serve every board.
16. **`assign_nets --check` appends its section to `reports/assign_nets.txt`**, so the committed report
   depends on whether check_all ran after make_pcb. Board-local: `make_pcb.sh --check` compares only the
   stage A/B part (`sed '/^== after zone fill/,$d'`). The shared tool would need: the `--check` section
   in its own file (or not written by default).
17. **`make_pcb.sh` per board** (tube_psu's with the pour steps and LCSC removed, the check directory
   in `build/tmp`): a shared `p2k make_pcb [--check]` with the step list from the config (pours or
   not) would save the copy.
18. `build_pcb` prints "53 nets" (the kicadxml netlist incl. the off-board OT / VA_PRIME nets); the board
   has 47 (43 IPC + 4 pin-6). Cosmetic.

## Decisions

- Sheet names (no title blocks in the PDF): Output stage / Bias regulator / Bias supply, chosen
  by the agent from the content; the user can rename them (only `p2k_config.py`, before
  `make_skeleton`).
- KiCad refdes for the terminals with signs/spaces/dots/quote: table above (agent's choice, same
  convention as tube_psu). The off-board VA' terminal is `VA_PRIME` (same rule as `VS_PRIME`).
- Phase 1 preparation (agent's choices, no user decision needed): all 29 terminals use one symbol
  `PIN` (Connector:TestPoint, as tube_psu); OT is an off-board box symbol with the Proteus pin
  names; EL34 keeps KiCad's two units (heater separate); the named nets keep the IPC spelling
  (`HT +6` with a space, `VS'` with a quote: labels work in KiCad 10, tube_psu tested `-100V BIAS`).
- Phase 1 transcription (agent's choices, no user decision needed): sheet 1 heaters as a separate
  block and the cathode row below the tubes (KiCad EL34 symbol shape; nets unchanged); EL34 anode
  pin type `passive`; A3 sheets with the PDF geometry at 1:1 on the 1.27 mm grid.
- Phase 2 (agent's choices, no user decision needed): KiCad pad sizes (`FOOTPRINT_PAD_SIZES =
  "kicad"`, the playbook default) with the pad centres on the Proteus positions; derived footprints
  where KiCad has the package at another pitch (5 W and 2 W resistors, film cap, C6, TO-92) – the 5 W
  resistor is derived from KiCad's L25 x 6.4 mm box rather than built from the IPC pads, because the
  box fits the Proteus silk and keeps the 3D model; built from the IPC pads: octal socket (pitch circle
  differs), fly-lead terminal; octal footprint keeps the Proteus pin ids and the top side (Findings 10);
  footprint names describe the Proteus geometry (`_P30.00mm`, `_P9.70mm`, `_PM` = pads +/-, `_AK`).
- Phase 3 (agent's choices, no user decision needed): `PCB_THICKNESS` 1.6 mm (2010 standard; the
  2026 READ-ME stack-up of 1.555 mm is Proteus 8's default); `PCB_RULES` from the measured Proteus
  copper (clearance 0.38 under the 0.381 mm smallest gap, edge 0.5, hole-to-hole 1.0; via values are
  placeholders); KiCad pad sizes kept (DRC needs no Proteus pads); courtyard overlaps and the VBx PTH
  in the socket courtyards accepted (`DRC_ACCEPTED`); `make_pcb.sh` without `place_refdes` (Phase 5).
- **Open for the user** (non-blocking): which side the octal sockets are mounted on (Findings 10);
  in Phase 4: C1-C6 KiCad drills 1.0 vs 1.143 mm (`DRILL_SMALLER` or Proteus drills). The R24/R25
  pad question is closed by Phase 3: the KiCad 2.4 mm pads keep 1.086 mm to the nearest copper, DRC clean.
